Race Preview · Round 10

Monza: the season restarts, and Tsolov holds a twenty-point lead.

By Nikolay Tomov · Published 29 August 2026 · 6 min read

The summer break ends where speed is worshipped. Round 10 of the 2026 F2 season is at the Autodromo Nazionale Monza, the fastest circuit on the calendar, and Nikola Tsolov arrives still leading the championship — 167 points, 20 clear of Gabriele Minì and 22 ahead of Rafael Câmara. Six weeks of nothing to race have not changed the order at the top; they have only given his rivals time to think about how far back they are.

Budapest was the weekend built to hurt the leader, and it barely did. Monza is the opposite kind of track, and that matters more than the break.

Two men are still in this: Minì on 147 and Câmara on 145. Dunne, 59 back, needs a collapse rather than a comeback. With five rounds and a maximum of roughly 165 points still on the table, the lead is comfortable but not safe — a bad Tsolov weekend paired with a Câmara win could halve it in two days. The pattern to hold onto is the one from Budapest: score what the day allows, and let the other two split the points behind him.

Tsolov knows Monza — from Formula 3

This is his first Formula 2 start at Monza, but not his first time racing here. His Monza history runs through F3, and it is a good one:

  • 2023, FIA F3 (rookie): Monza was where he scored his first F3 points, one of the bright spots of a difficult first season.
  • 2025, FIA F3 (finale): he finished second in the Monza Feature Race, sealing the drivers' runner-up spot and helping Campos Racing clinch its first F3 teams' title at the season's final round. A podium at the temple, on the weekend it mattered most.

The F2 car is heavier and faster, and the tyre and slipstream game is its own puzzle. But Tsolov will not arrive at Monza learning the corners, and the same Campos race pace that has been the class of the field all season should travel well to a power circuit.

Why Monza races nothing like Budapest

At the Hungaroring, track position was everything and overtaking was close to impossible — which is exactly why a P12 start there was such a problem. Monza is the reverse. It is a low-downforce circuit of long straights and hard braking zones, where the slipstream is worth tenths and DRS trains form naturally into the first chicane and into the Roggia. Places change hands here.

That cuts both ways for a championship leader. Qualifying still sets up the weekend, and a clean Friday matters — but a grid mistake is far less costly than it was in Budapest, and so is a safety car. The flip side is chaos: Monza's first-chicane braking zone is one of the most incident-prone corners of the year, and the slipstream lottery can shuffle a Sprint grid in a single lap. For a driver protecting a 20-point lead, the first lap is the risk, not the pace.

What a good weekend looks like

Survive lap one, and the maths keeps working.

Tsolov does not need to win Monza. He needs to qualify near the front, keep the car clean through the first-chicane scramble, and take the points that a slipstream circuit hands to a fast car in clean air. If Minì and Câmara beat each other, even a quiet podium extends the lead toward the point where five rounds becomes four with the gap intact.

The genuine risk is not pace, it is the opening lap — the one place at Monza where a title can wobble before the tyres are even warm. Get through it, and this is a track where his and Campos's strengths line up. Lights out Sunday at 09:45 local, just before the Italian Grand Prix.

Frequently asked questions

When is the F2 Monza 2026 round?

4–6 September 2026 at the Autodromo Nazionale Monza. Free Practice and Qualifying are on Friday, the Sprint Race on Saturday afternoon, and the Feature Race on Sunday morning before the Italian Grand Prix. It is the first round after the summer break.

Has Nikola Tsolov raced at Monza before?

Yes, in FIA Formula 3. He scored his first F3 points at Monza in 2023, and finished second in the 2025 Monza Feature Race — the F3 finale — where Campos took the teams' title and he sealed the drivers' runner-up. This is his first F2 start at the circuit.

What is the F2 championship situation going into Monza?

Tsolov leads on 167 points, 20 clear of Minì (147) and 22 ahead of Câmara (145), with Dunne fourth on 108. Five rounds remain after Monza.

Why does Monza race differently from Budapest?

Monza is a low-downforce track of long straights and heavy braking, so slipstreaming and DRS make overtaking much easier than at the Hungaroring, where track position was everything. A deep grid slot is far less costly here — but the first-chicane braking zone makes lap one the real risk.
